# ADR-0048: Reflection EPC minimum segment length and probe-first plane-group containment

- Status: Accepted; pending review by Xingyu Chen
- Date: 2026-11-11
- Decision ID: `epc-validity-and-probe-containment`
- Scope: `include/rayd/reflection.h` (`ReflectionEpcRequest::min_segment_length`),
  `src/reflection/reflection_internal.h` (`ReflEpcParams::min_segment_length`),
  `src/reflection/reflection_algorithms.cuh` (validity check and plane-group containment of
  `run_reflection_epc_algo`), `src/reflection/reflection.cpp` (request validation), and the
  regenerated `generated/drjit/ptx/reflection_epc_ptx.h`.

## Context

Channel's plan item M3-1 (finding C-1) defines one validity rule set for specular reflection paths,
applied by one validator, the reflection EPC. Two cases need RayD:

1. Degenerate chains. A chain whose consecutive reflection points coincide (two facades meeting at
   an edge, both points on the edge; or the same plane twice) passed the EPC: the back-trace
   accepts a zero-length span, and segment visibility treats any segment shorter than
   `kMinSegmentLength` (2e-5 m) as unobstructed. Channel's two candidate schemes produced
   different sets of these rows, so their results differed.
2. Plane-group containment was a linear scan (finding P-9). `point_inside_surface_group` tested
   the expected group's members one by one, so subdividing a ground plane from 2 to 131,072
   triangles slowed a depth-2 EPC launch from 4.5 ms to 768 ms with an identical answer.

## Decision

**Minimum segment length.** `ReflectionEpcRequest` gains `double min_segment_length = 0.0`
(`ReflEpcParams::min_segment_length`). After the back-trace, a lane whose source-to-first-point,
point-to-point or last-point-to-receiver segment is shorter than it is invalid. The default 0
keeps every existing caller's result bit for bit; the legacy dispatcher, the field EPC and the
Dr.Jit host code zero-initialize the parameter. The request validates it as finite and
non-negative. The caller chooses the threshold (Channel uses a fraction of its scene diagonal).

**Probe-first containment.** For a bounce with a plane group, the kernel traces a short segment
along the bounce's plane normal through the reflection point, from `p + h n` to `p - h n` with
`h = 1e-4 * max(|p|_inf, 1)`, through the same closest-hit trace the discovery mode uses (both
tracers, OptiX and pure CUDA, one shared body). When the closest triangle met belongs to the
expected group and `point_inside_triangle` accepts the point, that triangle is the resolved
primitive. Otherwise the existing exact member scan decides. The accepted set is therefore exactly
the scan's: a probe hit is confirmed by the same triangle test, and every probe miss is re-decided
by the scan. Only the resolved primitive of a point that two members share (an internal edge of
the group) may change, which does not change the path, its plane, or its validity.

`h` spans the coplanar-group quantization (1e-4 m in Channel) for coordinates of at least 1 m, so
every member of a group crosses the probe segment.

## Not decided here

- The member table stays padded (`group_count * max_group_size`). Converting it to CSR changes the
  public request layout of the Torch typed API, the legacy dispatcher and the Dr.Jit
  `ReflEpcOptions`; with probe-first containment the table is read only for points the probe does
  not resolve, and Channel removes most of those before the EPC with its own plane-group occupancy
  test. A CSR layout is left to a later record.
- Channel's coupled R-D/D-R geometry keeps `min_segment_length = 0`.

## Evidence

- `tests/native/integration_test.cpp` checks that a 0.5 m minimum keeps, and a 1.5 m minimum
  rejects, a path with two 1 m segments.
- The Dr.Jit `reflection_epc` PTX was regenerated from the edited sources
  (`RAYD_REGENERATE_REFLECTION_EPC_PTX=ON`) and copied over the committed header; the
  `reflection_trace` and `reflection_accumulation` PTX regenerated byte-identical (they do not
  reach the edited code). `drjit/ptx_sources.json` was rewritten from the regenerated sources.
- Channel (plan M3-1 to M3-8): on the 4x4 synthetic city subdivided to 41,472 triangles, depth-1
  reflection with 16,384 receivers runs in 7.7 ms (0.5.0: 51 ms), and the unsubdivided scene in
  5.9 ms (0.5.0: 13.6 ms), with identical path sets; the brute-force completeness tests of the
  Channel accuracy suite pass at depths 1-4.

## Amendment: metric in-face slack (2026-11-11)

**Context.** Channel's full-wave reference suite (Channel `docs/dev/fullwave-validation.md`, defect D1)
found the reflection support of large faces running past their boundary edges. `point_inside_triangle`
accepted barycentrics down to `-kEpcTolerance = -1e-4`. A barycentric is a distance in units of the
opposite edge's altitude, so the slack was `1e-4` of the triangle size: 0.4 mm past the edge of a 4 m
screen and 4 mm past the edge of a 40 m one (walls and screens that stand for unbounded surfaces). The
reflection then switched off millimetres beyond the edge's reflection shadow boundary, where the UTD
reflection term of that edge switches, so the total field carried a step of nearly the full reflected
amplitude on every sample in between.

**Decision.** The slack is a distance: a point is inside when it lies at most
`slack = 8 * 2^-23 * max(|vertices|_inf, |point|_inf)` outside each edge line, that is
`b_i * 2A >= -slack * |e_i|` for each barycentric `b_i` and opposite edge `e_i`. The slack is a few
float roundings of the containment arithmetic at the coordinates involved, so a point on an edge
shared by two coplanar triangles is still accepted by at least one of them, while a point past a
boundary edge is rejected at every face size. `kEpcTolerance` keeps its other use (the segment
parameter tolerance of `intersect_line_plane`). The rule is shared by the discovery containment, the
probe-first plane-group containment and the field EPC, so every reflection validator and Channel's
reflection-diffraction chain legs use it; Channel's path solver mirrors it in its own candidate test.

**Evidence.**
- `tests/reflection/test_torch_epc_face_containment.py`: on a 40 m plate, receivers whose reflection
  point lies 0.05-1 mm past the edge are rejected and those 0.05-1 mm inside are accepted, on both
  tracers. With the former slack all six are accepted.
- Channel full-wave gate (two PEC screens infinite in y, Channel defect D1): RSB p95 jump excess
  4.32 -> -0.03 dB (z polarization), 6.25 -> 2.84 dB (y polarization; the rest is not containment);
  the RSB transition region 2.26 -> 1.03 dB RMS.
- The `reflection_epc` PTX was regenerated and copied over the committed header; `reflection_trace`
  and `reflection_accumulation` regenerated byte-identical. `tests/reflection` (60) and `tests/parity`
  (100, 18 skipped) pass.
